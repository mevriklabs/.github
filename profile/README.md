# Mevrik

**The Agentic eXperience (AX) Platform.**

AI runs customer experience. Humans supervise what matters.

Mevrik AX is the AI-first customer experience platform: AI agents that resolve support, sales, and engagement conversations end-to-end — across web chat, WhatsApp, email, voice, and social — with human supervision built into every conversation.

- **AI agents that resolve, not deflect** — end-to-end conversation handling, from first message to outcome
- **Humans in command** — supervisors can listen, guide, or take over any conversation, live
- **Every channel, one platform** — web chat, WhatsApp, email, SMS, voice, and social
- **Enterprise-grade trust** — layered safety, auditability, and governance by design
