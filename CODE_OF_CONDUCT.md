# Code of Conduct

Mevrik is built by a small team that hopes to become a larger one. The culture we set now is the culture we live with later. This code applies in every repository, issue thread, pull request, code review, Slack channel, video call, and offsite.

## What we expect

- **Disagree on substance, never on people.** Argue about the code. Don't argue about the engineer.
- **Be precise.** Vague feedback wastes everyone's time. "This won't scale" without a reason is noise. "This loads all rows into memory; with our largest tenant it will OOM at ~3 GB" is signal.
- **Default to giving credit.** When in doubt, say someone's name. When you find a bug, credit the person who reported it. When you ship a feature, credit the reviewer who caught the regression.
- **Ask before assuming.** Every culture, language, time zone, and life situation on the team is different. If something reads wrong, ask before reacting.
- **Protect focus.** No `@channel` without a real reason. No "quick question" DMs to people who are heads-down. Use threads.
- **Show your work.** Decisions belong in ADRs, design docs, or PR descriptions — not in Slack history nobody can find.
- **Take feedback well.** A review is not an attack. A bug report is not a personal failing.
- **Give feedback well.** "Why did you do it this way?" lands better than "this is wrong."

## What we don't tolerate

- Harassment, discrimination, or hostility based on any personal attribute — including but not limited to race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, or appearance.
- Sexualised language or imagery in any work context.
- Personal insults, ad hominem attacks, doxxing, or threats.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Knowingly false claims about code, decisions, or other people.

These will result in a private conversation. Repeated or severe behaviour will result in removal from the project. There is no warning quota — severe single incidents are sufficient.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this code:

- **First option** — talk to the person directly if you feel safe to. Most issues are misunderstandings.
- **Second option** — email **conduct@mevrik.com**. The founder (Prince Mojumder) reads this. If the issue involves the founder, email **conduct-escalation@mevrik.com**, which routes to an external advisor.

Reports are confidential by default. We will not retaliate against good-faith reports, even if the reported behaviour is found not to violate this code.

## Enforcement

The founder makes enforcement decisions. As the team grows we'll establish a small enforcement panel. Actions, in escalating order:

1. Private conversation.
2. Written warning.
3. Temporary suspension from a repo or channel.
4. Removal from the project.

Severe violations skip steps.

## Scope

This applies to all `mevriklabs` repositories, official Mevrik communication channels, and any public event where someone represents Mevrik. It does not apply to private interactions between team members outside work — but if those interactions affect work, this code applies.
